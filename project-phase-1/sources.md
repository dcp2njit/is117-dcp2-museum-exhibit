## Licensing Checklist

Before final use, confirm:
1. Who owns the original media or screenshot.
2. Whether the asset is CC-licensed, public domain, or requires permission.
3. Whether educational fair use applies for a low-resolution, cited image.
4. Whether the citation includes author or site name and a stable URL.
