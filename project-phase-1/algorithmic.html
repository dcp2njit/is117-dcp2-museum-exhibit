Signal Flow

Every click, pause, and share becomes a ranking signal. The feed is reordered in real time to optimize engagement and attention.

  • Input: engagement signals

  • Rank: predicted value

Era Commentary

Algorithmic feeds turned design into continuous optimization. Interfaces are built to be tested, tracked, and tuned, often favoring clarity and repetition over novelty.

This era prioritizes measurable outcomes, making visual systems feel modular, standardized, and relentlessly efficient.

Design Impact

Data-driven interfaces reward speed and retention, pushing typography and layout toward repeatable, high-performing patterns.
