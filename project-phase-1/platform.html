Design Tokens

Design tokens translated brand decisions into reusable rules. Color, spacing, and type scales became standardized so teams could ship consistent interfaces across products and devices.

  • Primary color: Platform Blue
  • Surface: Neutral 100
  • Radius: 12px
  • Spacing: 8pt scale

System components in use, via Wikimedia Commons.

Grid Discipline

Platform-era layouts leaned on strict grids and breakpoints. These systems enforced predictable structure and helped designers balance desktop and mobile contexts.

  • 12-column grid
  • 24px gutters
  • Responsive breakpoints

Responsive web layout across devices, via Wikimedia Commons.

Era Commentary

As platforms scaled, design systems replaced personal experimentation. Metro, Material, and flat design languages focused on clarity, speed, and consistency, making interfaces feel familiar across devices.

These rules delivered usability at scale, but also narrowed the visual range of everyday web design.

Design Impact

Standardization raised baseline usability and production speed, while reducing visual risk and individual flair.
